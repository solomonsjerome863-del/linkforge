5 Activation — win the first 10 minutes

The single biggest lever in this playbook. A signup who never crawls a site is a number, not a user. Activation = first applied suggestion.

First-session checklist (in-app)

  • Add site URL (no plugin needed — crawler handles WP/Shopify/Webflow/Ghost)
  • Crawl starts immediately, progress visible
  • Top 5 suggestions pre-sorted by score, one-click preview of source → target
  • Apply first suggestion with undo available (kill fear)
  • Confetti + “X more opportunities found” → invites second session

Instrumentation

  • Event: site_added, crawl_completed, suggestion_reviewed, suggestion_applied
  • Dashboard: activation funnel drop-offs (already partially visible in Admin → Signups/Active Sites)
  • Alert: any user stuck at site_added for 24h → email #2 rescue
  • Weekly review: where do 60% of non-activated signups die?
Kill friction ruthlessly: every field removed from signup, every minute cut from crawl time, and every default (pre-selected site type, sample suggestions) is worth more than any ad spend this quarter.

7 Retention & expansion

Churn is silent until it isn't. Watch for no-login in 14 days and zero applies in a week — both get automated saves before any human touch.

Keep delivering between visits

  • Weekly digest: suggestions found / applied / Est. link equity gained
  • Monthly SEO report PDF (shareable — agencies forward to clients = free marketing)
  • Citation alerts: “Your brand was mentioned on site — no backlink yet”
  • Re-crawl nudge after content updates

Expansion plays

  • Pro user adding 6th site → Business pitch with price-per-site math
  • Agency digest shows client results → upgrade for white-label
  • Annual plan offer at month 3 of healthy usage
  • Referral: give 1 month, get 1 month (both sides Pro features)

10 30 / 60 / 90-day action plan

Days 0–30 Prove it

  • Instrument funnel events + weekly dashboard review (Mon)
  • Personal close 10 seed users from own network — founder emails, not ads
  • Communities: 5 genuine answers/week
  • Cold email: 20 agencies/week, track reply rate
  • Ship welcome sequence (§8)

Days 31–60 Scale what worked

  • Publish 4 SEO articles (internal linking cluster)
  • Ship the free scanner tool as lead magnet
  • Record + embed YouTube demo
  • Launch (Product Hunt / directories) once activation > 35%
  • First case study from a paying user

Days 61–90 Compound

  • Sign 2 partnership deals (WP agencies / hosting)
  • Annual plans live; offer to healthy accounts
  • Double the best-CAC channel, kill the worst
  • Referral program live
  • Review pricing against real usage data

11 Objection handling

ObjectionResponse
“Link Whisper is cheaper.”“It's WordPress-only and suggestion-only. LinkForge covers every platform, ranks fixes by revenue impact, and monitors your AI citations — R825 covers what two tools would.”
“I already do internal links manually.”“Great — run one free crawl and compare. If we don't find 20 links you missed, close the tab.”
“How good are AI suggestions, really?”“Every suggestion shows source, target, anchor, and a relevance score before you apply — and you can undo. Judge it on the first 10, not the marketing.”
“Is my site data safe?”“We crawl only pages you add, store content to generate suggestions, and never publish anything. Delete a site and its data goes with it (cascade, confirmed in-app).”
“Why Paystack only?”“Built for South African businesses — Rands, no forex fees, card + EFT. Enterprise can invoice.”
“R825 is expensive.”“One hour of your time at even R500/hr costs more than a month of Pro — and it finds links every month, not once.”
